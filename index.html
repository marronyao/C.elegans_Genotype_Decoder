Structure explorer

Enter an array or a general expression. Semicolons separate expressions; + separates constructs; :: and _ separate fragments outside brackets. Pmtl-2 is standardised to mtl-2p. Possible missing hyphens need your confirmation.

Structural breakdown

Your results will appear here after you parse a genotype.

Gene names, promoter-associated gene names, gene-linked allele identifiers, transgene identifiers and candidate strain names are sent to Alliance of Genome Resources for English annotations. Protein candidates are queried in UniProtKB using the corresponding gene name. Promoters show corresponding gene expression as a reference, not a verified promoter-fragment pattern. Known fluorescent proteins and selected transformation markers have local annotations. UTRs and selected amyloid-beta peptides have source-backed offline annotations. Non-fluorescent tags are not annotated. Common strains, fluorescent proteins and selected markers have source-backed offline summaries; other database lookups require an internet connection. The example is illustrative and has not been checked against a strain record.
